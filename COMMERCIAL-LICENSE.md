## Which one applies

**The AGPL is likely to suit you** if you are running Rayar RAG for yourself,
inside your own organisation, for research or teaching, or if you are willing
to publish the source of any modified version you make available to others
over a network.

**A commercial licence is likely to be necessary** if you intend to offer
Rayar RAG, or a product built on it, to your own users as a hosted service
without publishing your modifications; if you wish to distribute it inside a
proprietary product; or if your organisation's policy prohibits AGPL
dependencies.

This is a summary written for orientation, not a legal opinion. Where the two
differ, the AGPL text governs. If you are unsure which applies to your
situation, ask a lawyer, or write to us and we will tell you plainly whether
we think you need one.

## What a commercial licence includes

- The right to use, modify and distribute Rayar RAG without the AGPL's
  source-disclosure obligations
- Support terms and response times agreed in writing
- Assistance with deployment and configuration

Terms depend on the size of the deployment and what support is wanted.

pull request is opened.
